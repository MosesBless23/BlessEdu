Moses Mendy

Basketball Enthusiast | Author | Youth Mentor

Moses Mendy is a passionate basketball enthusiast based in Brikama Jamisa, The Gambia. He’s the author of a growing basketball guidebook, dedicated to shaping champions both on and off the court. With a focus on skill development, mindset, and leadership, Moses inspires the next generation through the power of sports and education. His work combines knowledge, discipline, and community empowerment — all rooted in the belief that greatness starts within.
